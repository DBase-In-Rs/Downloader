DBase Video & Music Downloader saves video and audio from any of the 1,750+ websites supported by yt-dlp, with FFmpeg handling MP3/M4A conversion and MP4 merging. Both tools are bundled — no setup needed.

<b>Features</b>

* Paste, type, or share a media URL from other apps (Android share sheet)
* Metadata preview: title, uploader, duration, and all available formats
* Output as MP3, M4A, MP4, or the original source format; video-only picks automatically merge the best audio track
* Playlists, albums, channels, and profiles: select items and add them to the queue in bulk
* Sequential download queue with pause/resume, retry, cancel, and persistence across restarts
* Live progress with speed, ETA, and stage; downloads continue in the background through a foreground service
* Download history with search, provider filter, open/share actions, and per-item delete
* Saves through Android MediaStore or a user-selected folder (SAF)
* Encrypted cookies.txt import for login-required media, with an in-app export guide, expired-cookie detection, and one-tap delete
* Manual yt-dlp engine update from Settings

<b>Privacy</b>

The app is fully local — no accounts, no analytics, no tracking or ad SDKs, and no project-operated backend. Cookies are encrypted at rest (Android Keystore), passed only to yt-dlp requests, never uploaded anywhere, and deletable from Settings at any time. Network requests go only to the media providers you download from (plus GitHub when you manually update the yt-dlp engine).
