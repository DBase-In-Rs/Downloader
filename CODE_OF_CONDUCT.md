# Code of Conduct

## Our Standards

This project should be a practical and respectful place to collaborate.

Examples of expected behavior:

- be direct, technical, and respectful;
- assume mistakes are fixable;
- keep criticism focused on code, design, documentation, or process;
- respect privacy and security concerns, especially around cookies and account
  data.

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- harassment, threats, insults, or discriminatory language;
- publishing another person's private information;
- pressuring contributors to share credentials, cookies, private media, or
  private links;
- encouraging copyright infringement, DRM bypass, or unauthorized access.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, lock, or remove comments, issues, pull requests, or
other contributions that violate this code of conduct. Repeated or severe
violations may lead to a ban from project spaces.

## Reporting

Report conduct issues through GitHub's private contact options when available.
If private reporting is not configured yet, contact the maintainer through a
non-public channel before opening a public issue.
